09 / Limitations

Important Notes

Review the renderer, animation, and baked-material considerations before using Metalworks in production.

01

Designed Primarily for Cycles

Metalworks was developed primarily for the Cycles renderer. The full procedural effect will not work correctly in EEVEE because some of the required procedural shading features are not supported there. For EEVEE, first bake the material in Cycles and then use the generated texture maps in the baked PBR material.

02

Animated and Deforming Objects

The procedural material is not recommended for objects whose mesh deforms or changes during animation. On rigged characters, shape keys, cloth, or other deforming geometry, the procedural surface may be recalculated as the mesh moves. This can cause rust, wear, dirt, or other effects to shift or appear in places where they were not visible in the original pose.

For animated characters and deforming objects, bake the material before animation or final rendering. The baked texture maps remain fixed to the UV layout and preserve the surface appearance while the object moves.

03

Baked Result Adjustments

The baked material is designed to match the procedural result as closely as possible, but small differences may remain in Specular, Roughness, Bump, and Normal Maps.

Minor adjustments to Bump Height, Normal Map Strength, Roughness, or Specular Correction may be required. See the One-Click Baking comparison above for a direct example.
